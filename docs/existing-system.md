# Existing System

The existing healthcare system relies on traditional methods and multiple independent digital platforms to manage healthcare-related activities. Patients often need to visit healthcare facilities physically or use different platforms to access medical services and information.

In many cases, appointment booking, doctor consultation, healthcare information, and patient records are managed separately. This makes the overall healthcare experience time-consuming and less convenient.

## Limitations of the Existing System

1. **Time-Consuming Process:** Patients may need to spend considerable time visiting healthcare facilities for basic services.

2. **Lack of Centralization:** Healthcare services and information are often distributed across different platforms instead of being available in one place.

3. **Appointment Management Issues:** Traditional appointment scheduling methods can create difficulties in managing appointments efficiently.

4. **Limited Accessibility:** Patients may face difficulties accessing healthcare services remotely.

5. **Communication Gap:** Communication between patients and healthcare professionals may become difficult when services are not properly integrated.

6. **Record Management Challenges:** Maintaining and accessing patient information through traditional methods can be inconvenient.

## Conclusion

Although existing healthcare systems provide essential medical services, the lack of integration, accessibility, and centralized management creates several challenges. These limitations highlight the need for a digital healthcare solution like MediConnect that aims to make healthcare services more organized and accessible.
