# Objectives and Scope

## Objectives

The main objective of MediConnect is to develop a digital healthcare platform that makes healthcare services more accessible, convenient, and organized for users.

The major objectives of the project are:

1. **Easy Access to Healthcare:** To provide users with a convenient platform to access healthcare-related services.

2. **Doctor Connectivity:** To help patients connect with doctors through the platform.

3. **Appointment Management:** To simplify the process of booking and managing doctor appointments.

4. **Centralized Platform:** To bring different healthcare-related functionalities together in one system.

5. **User-Friendly Interface:** To develop a simple and easy-to-use interface for patients and healthcare professionals.

6. **Efficient Information Management:** To organize healthcare-related information in a structured manner.

7. **Time Saving:** To reduce unnecessary effort and make accessing healthcare services more convenient.

## Scope of the Project

MediConnect aims to provide a digital platform that connects patients with healthcare services through an organized and user-friendly system.

The scope of the project includes:

- **Patient Module:** Allows patients to register, log in, and access available healthcare services.
- **Doctor Module:** Provides doctors with access to their relevant functionalities and patient-related information.
- **Appointment Module:** Supports appointment booking and management.
- **Healthcare Information:** Provides access to relevant healthcare-related information.
- **Admin Module:** Helps manage users and oversee the platform.
- **Centralized Access:** Brings the supported healthcare functionalities together in a single platform.

## Future Scope

In the future, MediConnect can be enhanced by adding features such as online payment integration, video consultations, electronic medical records, AI-based health assistance, and mobile application support.

These improvements can make the platform more advanced and useful for a wider range of healthcare requirements.
