# Problem Statement

## 1. Introduction

Hospitals manage various activities every day, including patient registration, doctor scheduling, appointment management, medical record maintenance and medicine inventory management.

In many healthcare institutions, these activities may be handled through manual processes or separate systems. This can make information management difficult and time-consuming.

## 2. Identified Problems

The major problems identified in traditional hospital management processes are:

### 2.1 Manual Record Management
Maintaining patient information manually can make storing, updating and retrieving records difficult.

### 2.2 Appointment Management Difficulties
Manual appointment scheduling may lead to delays, scheduling conflicts and difficulties in managing doctor availability.

### 2.3 Disorganized Medical Records
Patient medical information may be stored in different places, making it difficult to access previous consultation details.

### 2.4 Medicine Inventory Management
Tracking medicine quantities and expiry dates manually can increase the chances of errors and stock-related problems.

### 2.5 Lack of Centralized Information
When different hospital activities are managed separately, accessing updated information becomes challenging.

## 3. Proposed Solution

MediConnect proposes a centralized web-based Hospital Management System to address these problems.

The application aims to provide a common platform for patients, doctors and administrators.

It will support patient registration, doctor management, appointment scheduling, medical record maintenance and medicine inventory monitoring.

## 4. Expected Benefits

- Better organization of hospital information.
- Easier appointment management.
- Improved accessibility of patient records.
- Organized medicine inventory tracking.
- Reduced dependence on manual paperwork.
- Better coordination between different hospital users.

## 5. Conclusion

MediConnect aims to simplify essential hospital management activities through a centralized digital platform. It provides an organized approach to managing hospital information and supports the gradual digitalization of routine healthcare administrative processes.
