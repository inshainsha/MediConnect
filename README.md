# 🏥 MediConnect – Smart Hospital Management System

### Connecting Healthcare Through Technology

MediConnect is a web-based Hospital Management System designed to simplify and organize essential hospital operations through a centralized digital platform.

It aims to connect patients, doctors and administrators by bringing important healthcare management activities together in one system.

## 📌 About the Project

Hospitals manage a large amount of information every day, including patient details, doctor schedules, appointments, medical records and medicine inventory.

MediConnect aims to address these challenges by providing a unified platform where different hospital activities can be managed efficiently.

The system is designed with separate role-based interfaces for patients, doctors and administrators.

## ❓ Why MediConnect?

Traditional hospital management processes may involve:

- Manual maintenance of patient records.
- Time-consuming appointment scheduling.
- Difficulty accessing historical medical information.
- Disconnected information management.
- Challenges in tracking medicine stock and expiry dates.

MediConnect aims to simplify these activities through centralized digital management.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Digitize essential hospital management activities.
- Simplify patient registration and information management.
- Improve doctor appointment scheduling.
- Maintain organized medical records.
- Monitor medicine availability and expiry dates.
- Provide role-based access to authorized users.
- Improve the efficiency of routine administrative operations.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

MediConnect follows a layered architecture to maintain separation of responsibilities.

1. **Presentation Layer:** Handles user interaction through web pages.
2. **Controller Layer:** Receives and processes HTTP requests.
3. **Service Layer:** Contains business logic and application operations.
4. **Repository Layer:** Handles database communication.
5. **Database Layer:** Stores application information using MySQL.

This structure helps improve maintainability, organization and scalability.
